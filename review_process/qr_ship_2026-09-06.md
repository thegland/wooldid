### 6.2 The program-size limit (new standing constraint)

`wooldid_worker` sits at Stata's per-program compiled-size limit: adding about 1 KB of code
(comments are free) makes the ado fail to load with r(1000) "system limit exceeded" at the
worker's `end`. The BS-8 fix hit it first. Measured with padded scratch copies: at the BS-26
commit the headroom was between 0.8 and 1.1 KB; after the refactor (dd4e85f) it is between
3.6 and 5.4 KB (worker code bytes 151,380). New worker logic therefore goes into subprograms
(`wooldid_sglbl`, `wooldid_icscreen`), with r() consumed by the caller before anything
rclass runs. `wooldid_validate` has room.
