# Rank-1 e(V_es_ame) investigation - report

Read-only analysis of /Users/hegland/Documents/Research/wooldid_dev/wooldidinstall/wooldid.ado
(bugfixes branch) plus empirical probes in this directory (probe1-probe9 .do/.log). 2026-08-24.

## Verdict

**DEFECT (of the reported covariance object, not of the margins/F2 plumbing): the true
sampling covariance of the per-horizon AME estimates is NOT rank 1; the cluster-robust V that
wooldid reports annihilates every horizon-specific variance component by construction.**
The reported (b, V) pair is self-refuting: it asserts that every cross-horizon contrast
b_h - b_h' is estimated with exactly zero sampling variance, while the point estimates of
those contrasts are nonzero and vary freely across 120 iid-error Monte Carlo replications
of the same design (and must do so under ANY error model with nondegenerate within-cell
idiosyncratic noise, since the within-cell slope s_gh always carries sampling variance). Mechanically, with contreat() + contreatcontrols(basic) every treated
(cohort g x relative-period h) cell carries its own visible intercept AND its own visible
dose slope, so the OLS normal equations force sum(u)=0 and sum(dose*u)=0 within every
treated cell (verified to 6e-14). When the VCE is clustered at the cohort level (cluster(i),
i = the cohort argument), each cell lies inside a single cluster, so the plug-in CRVE score
of every cell-slope coefficient is exactly zero and the entire slope block of e(V) collapses
to Var(phi_hat)*ones, phi_hat being the single pooled baseline-dose coefficient (the
absorbed "ringer"). Every AME quantity then inherits variance Var(phi_hat) and correlation
1: rank-1 V_es_ame, identical SEs on every horizon, on the main ame, and on the pretreatment
ame. The delta-method chain itself is faithful (r(b) = J*b_model to 1e-16 and
r(V) = J*e(V)*J' exactly; the Jacobian rows are horizon-specific and correct), and the same
rank-1 V reproduces under plain `regress, vce(cluster i)` - it is the CRVE math (incidental
cell parameters saturating each cluster), not a reghdfe or margins bug. The Monte Carlo
truth benchmark shows the actual sampling covariance of the 8 AMEs is full rank with
per-horizon variances about 5x the reported common value. The adversarial refusal
("composite-null covariance is degenerate") is the correct response GIVEN that V; the defect
is upstream, in computing/posting that V as the ES-ame covariance under cohort-level
clustering. On unclustered configs the same estimator/V pipeline is internally sound
(full-rank homoskedastic V; the fixture's identical SEs are a benign balanced-design
artifact, not the defect).

## Mechanism (traced code)

All cites into wooldidinstall/wooldid.ado (bugfixes branch):

- L906: `gen double ringer = contreat` - an alias of dose under a different name,
  deliberately "invisible to margins" (comment block L898-905).
- L907, L918: `contreatpolypart = c.dose`; `contreatinj = <lattice>##c.dose` (no lattice =>
  `##c.dose`).
- L1017-1020: contreatcontrols(basic), no lattice => `contreatcontrolinserterFE =
  i.null##c.(ringer)` with null==1 - the pooled baseline dose slope, pushed into absorb().
- L1099: the regression (verbose-verified, probe1.log):
  `reghdfe y i._i#i._t#i.targetflag##c.dose, absorb(i.i i.t i.null##c.(ringer)) cluster(i)`.
  `##c.dose` gives every (g,h,targetflag) cell BOTH a dummy D_gh and a cell-specific dose
  slope S_gh = D_gh*dose; the common c.dose main effect is dropped as collinear with the
  absorbed ringer.
- L2186: the ES-ame margins call: `margins [iw=w_att], dydx(dose) subpop(if targetflag==1 &
  _t in [horizonstarter,horizonend]) noestimcheck over(_t) df(df_r)`.
- L2195-2200: F2 captures r(b)/r(V) raw; L3360-3361 posts them unchanged as
  e(b_es_ame)/e(V_es_ame). Downstream consumers (honestdid/lincom/test - the reason F2
  exists) therefore ingest the rank-1 V silently.
- L2223 + wooldid_advstat (L5570-5650) + mata wooldid_advx (L6331ff): the adversarial test
  whitens V on its positive-eigenvalue subspace; with rank(V)=1 the testable dimensions are
  q = 1 - rank(line design) = 0 => status "degen" => "composite-null covariance is
  degenerate; no p-value computed"; e(adversarial_pval_ame) not posted.
- L1915 (main ame) and L1962 (pretreatment ame): margins on the same model/VCE - same
  degeneracy (Task-3 section).

What margins differentiates: dydx(dose) sees the factor-notation interactions
`g._i#h._t#1.targetflag#c.dose`, so the per-observation derivative in cell (g,h) is gamma_gh
(the visible cell slope; the absorbed ringer slope phi is intentionally excluded - the AME
is the treatment-effect slope net of the baseline dose effect). over(_t) averages within
horizon h: b_h = sum_g w_gh*gammahat_gh (w_gh = cell share of the horizon-h subpop).
Empirically the Jacobian G has row h loading w_gh (1/20 or 1/25 in repro (a); 1/9 in the
fixture) on exactly the horizon-h slope columns - rows are horizon-specific and NOT
identical (probe2b.log, probe3.log). Consistency: max|G*bhat - r(b)| = 1.1e-16,
max|G*e(V)*G' - r(V)| = 0 (probe3b.log).

**So the rank-1 comes from e(V) itself.** probe3b.log: the full 195x195 block of the
clustered e(V) over all treated-cell slope coefficients is EXACTLY a constant matrix:
min entry = max entry = mean = 0.0296703778967, sd of entries 1.5e-14, eigenvalues
{195c, ~1e-15,...}. probe5.log identifies the constant: refitting with the ringer visible
gives Var_cluster(phihat) = 0.17225092^2 = 0.02967038 - the same number. Every reported ame
SE in repro (a) is 0.1722509 (probe3.log): all 8 ES horizons, main ame, pretreatment ame.

Why the CRVE collapses (exact argument; empirics in probe3b.log):

1. Each treated cell (g,h) is one cohort x one calendar period and carries visible D_gh and
   S_gh. Normal equations => within EVERY treated cell sum(uhat)=0 and sum(dose*uhat)=0
   (measured max |sum| = 6.2e-14 across all 195 cells).
2. By FWL, since cohort FE, time FE and the ringer are the only other regressors touching a
   cell (each cell is a single (g,t) block, so those are constant within it),
   gammahat_gh = s_gh - phihat EXACTLY, where s_gh is the raw within-cell OLS slope of y on
   dose (verified to ~1e-8 in probe6_small.log on a 4-cohort x 3-unit x 6-period example).
3. The plug-in CRVE meat uses cluster scores s_g = Xtilde_g'uhat_g. With cluster = cohort,
   the raw part of every cell-slope score is the cell's own sum(dose*uhat) = 0; what
   survives loads only on cluster-level moments shared through the absorbed space, and for
   the slope coefficients that is the single ringer direction. Hence for ALL pairs
   Cov_CRVE(gammahat_gh, gammahat_g'h') = Var_CRVE(phihat): the within-cell slope noise
   Var(s_gh) is invisible to the CRVE because the estimated residuals were purged of it - it
   went into the coefficient (the incidental-parameter / cluster-saturation pathology of
   plug-in CRVE; no cohort-level CRVE variant can recover it - CR2/CR3-type corrections
   divide by (I - H_gg), which is singular in exactly those directions).
4. Therefore V_es_ame = G e(V) G' = Var(phihat)*ones exactly: rank 1, equal diagonals,
   correlations 1 - while b_h = mean_g s_gh - phihat varies freely across h through the
   s_gh.

## Small-example math

Model actually fit (no lattice, poly 1, contreatcontrols(basic)); cells (g,h) = treated
cohort x relative period, each one calendar period with several obs and within-cell dose
variation; pooled control cell:

    y_it = alpha_g + lambda_t + phi*d_it
           + sum_{(g,h)} D_gh,it * (theta_gh + gamma_gh*d_it) + [control-cell terms] + u_it

(theta_gh, gamma_gh) saturate the cell and everything else is constant within it, so least
squares fits each treated cell by its own line:

    gammahat_gh + phihat = s_gh := Cov_cell(d,y)/Var_cell(d)      (exact; probe6_small)

Reported AME at horizon h:  b_h = sum_g w_gh*gammahat_gh = sum_g w_gh*s_gh - phihat.

TRUE sampling covariance (any error model with nondegenerate within-cell noise):

    Var(b_h)       = sum_g w_gh^2 Var(s_gh) + Var(phihat) - 2 sum_g w_gh Cov(s_gh, phihat)
    Cov(b_h,b_h')  = cross terms + Var(phihat)

with Var(s_gh) = sigma2_cell/(n_gh*Var_cell(d)) > 0, horizon-specific, s_gh essentially
independent across cells => full rank (diagonal-dominant + common rank-1 term). Under iid
errors this equals sigma2 * G(Xtilde'Xtilde)^{-1}G' - what the unclustered run reports.

CRVE with cluster = cohort estimates Var(s_gh) as 0 for every cell (step 3), leaving

    V_CRVE(b) = Var_CRVE(phihat) * ones      - rank 1, correlation 1, equal diagonals.

Numbers (repro (a): 30 cohorts x 6 units x 12 periods, obs-level dose, cluster(i)):
reported diagonals all 0.0296703779 (SE .1722509), corr 1.0, eigenvalues {.2373630,
~1e-17 x 7}; truth-shape (unadjusted VCE on the same fit; equals the true covariance under
the iid DGP up to s2~sigma2): diagonals .1316-.1703, off-diagonal corr ~.16-.19,
eigenvalues .336....108 - full rank. Reported per-horizon variances ~5x too small; reported
contrast variances 0 vs true ~2*.15*(1-.17) ~ .25.

## MC-design implication

Verdict = defect, so: the correct V for the ES-ame vector is one whose meat can see
within-cell residual variation. On these designs any of the following produce a sound,
full-rank V_es_ame and a well-posed adversarial ame p:

- no vce option (homoskedastic V; exactly right under iid errors - matches the MC truth in
  shape and scale);
- robust (HC meat uses per-observation residuals, which do not vanish within cells);
- clustering at any level strictly finer than the cohort x period cells (e.g. panel-unit
  clustering), so cells span multiple clusters (probe9);
- a cluster bootstrap of the AME functional (resample cohorts, recompute b) when
  cohort-level dependence must be honored - plug-in CRVE at cohort level can NEVER recover
  the within-cell component (the score is identically zero).

Is the adversarial ame p computed today (the timetrends suite config) trustworthy? Under
UNclustered configs - the only ones observed to post it - yes, in the same sense as any
homoskedastic-VCE inference: V is full rank and consistent under iid errors; the p is
well-posed. Under cohort-level clustering the p never posts (V rank 1 => q=0; probe7 run 3
checks that timetrends does not rescue it) - and it must not, because any p computed from
that V (as the old build did via g-inverse `test`) treats cross-horizon contrasts as
noiseless and is statistically meaningless. For MC studies: do not use cohort-level
cluster(i) configs to exercise adversarial-ame inference; use unclustered/robust or
unit-clustered configs, or a bootstrap benchmark.

## Suggested fix sketch

The margins/F2/adversarial plumbing is sound; the fix belongs at the VCE/reporting layer:

1. Detect and refuse (minimum fix). After the ES/main/pre ame margins calls, when the ame
   covariance is degenerate (cheap tests: cluster() at the cohort level with contreat, or
   max-min of r(V) entries below tol, or rank(r(V))==1 with >1 horizons), warn explicitly:
   "cluster(<i>) clusters at the treatment-cell level; the cluster-robust covariance of
   continuous-treatment effects is degenerate (all ame SEs collapse to the SE of the pooled
   baseline dose slope). Per-horizon ame SEs and cross-horizon inference are not identified
   by this VCE; use robust, a finer cluster variable, or a bootstrap." Suppress or flag
   e(V_es_ame) rather than posting a rank-1 matrix that downstream consumers (honestdid,
   lincom, test) will silently mis-use.
2. Do not display identical SEs as if informative: blank or footnote the ame SE column under
   this condition, mirroring the adversarial code's refusal.
3. Optional constructive fix: a design-based VCE for the ame quantities under cohort
   clustering: (a) score/cluster bootstrap of the margins functional, or (b) an analytic
   hybrid exploiting b_h = mean_g s_gh - phihat: Var(s_gh) estimable within cell
   (sigma2_gh/(n_gh Var_cell(d))), Var(phihat) from the CRVE, pieces asymptotically
   uncorrelated.
4. Documentation: note that cohort-level cluster(i) interacts pathologically with contreat's
   cell-saturated design; recommend unit-level clustering when the cohort variable
   aggregates multiple panel units (exactly the contreat use case, since dose must vary
   within cohort).
