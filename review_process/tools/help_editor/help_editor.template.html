This is wooldid.sthlp as Stata shows it, block by block. Click into any paragraph, heading or table cell and type; the block turns yellow and saves to the page's shared record as you go (the pill at the top right says who you are signed in as and whether the record is taking your edits). Italic, bold, code and underlined words can be edited in place. Links and other directives are fixed in this view; to change one, or to restructure a block, press Source on the block and edit its SMCL directly. Revert puts a block back to the shipped text. Copy my edits copies every changed block (original and new SMCL) to the clipboard for safekeeping. Make one small test edit first and check that its block says saved.
