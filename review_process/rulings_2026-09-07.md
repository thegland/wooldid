## F. PS-62 resolved: the analytic covariance (2026-09-07, after the six-lens review)

**Review verdict.** All six lenses returned *feasible in part*, with the same split. The author's
skepticism was right about the general matrix and wrong about the core case.

- **Already computed, nothing to estimate.** Every reported relative-period effect of a family comes
  from ONE `margins` call (ado 2023, 2502, 2711, 3066), so that call's `r(V)` is their complete joint
  covariance, in both reference regimes and for the subgroup twins. wooldid captures it (2034-2035,
  2520-2521, 2731-2732, 3088-3089) and posts it as `e(V_es_*)` (3884). It never reaches `e(V)`, which is
  overwritten with diag(se^2) at 3794. Row alignment with the reported rows is already enforced by the
  shared positional filter in `wooldid_sglbl` (10478-10488), so a splice is mechanical.
- **Cheap but unread.** The cross-subgroup level covariance sits in one call's `r(V)`; margins also
  returns the full pairwise contrast covariance `r(V_vs)`, of which wooldid reads only `r(table_vs)`.
- **Blocked, do not build.** The general cross-family matrix. Three modes have no delta-method object at
  all: `impvalues` (per-cluster t-test SEs under `nose`), `unconditionalse` (a linearization variance, not
  J V J'), and `fastnose` (no variance anywhere). Rank deficiency is guaranteed, not hypothetical: the
  main effect is a linear combination of the horizons, pinned at 6e-17 by identity_extras E4.
- **Comparator calibration.** wooldid is not uniquely deficient. One comparator ships diag(se^2) on its
  bootstrap path, another gives its per-cohort rows no covariance, a third keeps its aggregated event
  study out of `e()` entirely.

**Rulings.**

| question | ruling |
|---|---|
| scope | **Splice in the event-study blocks only.** The within-family event-study covariance goes into the corresponding diagonal blocks of `e(V)`; every other off-diagonal stays zero. No new estimation, and a test across horizons becomes valid. |
| labelling | **Help text plus a stored note.** Document the mixed matrix in the help, and store the warning as an `e()` macro. Deliberately NOT a footer under the printed table. |
| sentinel | **Zero the sentinel row and column.** Where a row's variance is unidentified, zero its row and column rather than carrying the -1e100 sentinel into a matrix with off-diagonals, which the posting screen can zero wholesale. |

**Consequence for PS-45 (simultaneous bands).** The condition the author attached is now met for the
event-study case specifically: the covariance those bands need exists on the analytic path and the full
replications-by-horizon draw matrix exists on the bootstrap path. Awaiting his go/no-go.
