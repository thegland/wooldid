## 1. The unified statistical account

The fitted model gives every treated (cohort g × relative-period h) cell its own visible intercept
θ_gh AND dose slope γ_gh, over an absorbed baseline dose block: pooled φ (basic), per-cohort φ_g
(byCohort), or per-calendar-period φ_t (byTime). By FWL, γ̂_gh = s_gh − φ̂_(block), where s_gh is
the raw within-cell OLS slope of y on dose (6 obs, 2 within-cell parameters). Every reported ame
quantity is an average b = Σ w_gh γ̂_gh, so its TRUE sampling variance has two components:

    Var(b) = Σ w² Var(s_gh)  [within-cell slope noise]  +  Var(φ̂-block term)  [baseline noise]

The whole 26-case table is generated by exactly two error channels acting on those components,
mixed by a composition weight (how many cells the quantity averages, and how noisy the baseline is):

- **Channel A — CRVE cell-score annihilation** (any cluster() at/above the cell level, i.e.
  cluster(i) here): the OLS normal equations zero the within-cell residual moments, so the plug-in
  CRVE score of every cell coefficient vanishes and the ENTIRE within-cell component is estimated as
  exactly 0. The reported V is purely the Var_cl of the absorbed baseline block, mapped through
  horizons. No cohort-level plug-in CRVE variant can recover the lost component (CR2/CR3 divide by a
  singular I−H_gg there, per inv1).
- **Channel B — plug-in leverage bias** (robust, or cluster strictly finer than cells): the meat
  sees within-cell residuals, but they are shrunk by fitting 2 parameters on 6 obs. Average leverage
  is 2/6; the slope direction weights high-|d̃| points, pushing effective slope leverage to
  ~0.40–0.45, so the within-cell variance component is estimated at ~0.55–0.65 of truth → SE ratios
  ~0.74–0.81 wherever that component dominates. This is textbook HC0/HC1 small-cell bias, made
  STRUCTURAL by the specification (the design guarantees tiny saturated cells).

Reported/true SE ratio ≈ sqrt[(V_base + κ·V_slope)/(V_base + V_slope)], κ = 0 (channel A) or
≈ 0.55–0.65 (channel B). V_slope ∝ 1/N_cells-averaged. Everything below is this formula.

### (a) d1/d2 + cluster(i): ES ratios ~0.2, main/pre ame ~0.4–0.5
Channel A with pooled φ: every one of the 10 reported SEs is the SAME number, SE_cl(φ̂) (inv1's
fingerprint; re-confirmed in probe 2 on d4). The ratio differs across quantities only through the
truth: an ES horizon averages ~15 cells → V_slope/15 dominates → ratio ~0.2 (V_base ≈ 4% of truth);
main ame averages ~105 post cells → slope noise ÷7 → ratio ~0.5; pretreatment ame ~60 cells → ~0.4.
Consistency check: (0.52/0.20)² ≈ 6.8 ≈ 105/15. The single posted number is a correct SE for
NOTHING; it merely covers a larger share of the truth for more-aggregated quantities.

### (b) byTime + cluster(i): full-rank V, still 0.38–0.69
**Probe 1 (run): each reported horizon SE equals the cluster-robust SE of the corresponding
per-calendar-period absorbed baseline slope φ̂_t EXACTLY (7 digits, all 8 horizons)** — e.g. rep-1
post0 SE .1719097 = SE_cl(φ̂_t=6) .1719097 from a visible `ibn.t#c.ringer` refit. Channel A is fully
intact; what changed is that the surviving baseline block is now period-specific, so the 8 horizons
inherit 8 DIFFERENT φ̂_t variances and covariances → V_es_ame is full rank (condition ~25–40, CSV
evmin/evmax median .026–.041) without containing one unit of within-cell slope noise. Ratios rise
from ~0.2 to ~0.4 (d1) / 0.56–0.69 (d2) only because Var(φ̂_t) (≈90 control obs per period) is ~4×
Var(pooled φ̂) (~1500 obs) — far flatter than the obs ratio because under cluster(i) precision is
limited by the 15 control clusters, not the obs count. Full rank is a spoofed health certificate here: the package's own
degeneracy screen (and the adversarial test) key on rank and will happily consume this V.

### (c) cluster(unitid)/robust on d1/d2: consistently ~0.70–0.91
Channel B. Horizon quantities are slope-noise-dominated (V_base ≈ 0 under cu: pooled φ̂ from ~1500
obs), so the ratio ≈ sqrt(κ) ≈ 0.74–0.81 — the observed 0.71–0.80 horizon band. Main ame is higher
(0.80–0.91) because its truth carries a diluted slope component. The magnitude implicates precisely
the estimator HC2/HC3 (CR2/CR3) exist for: 6-obs cells with 2 fitted within-cell parameters put
1/3 average leverage (0.40–0.45 slope-weighted) on every treated cell, biasing the plug-in meat down
~40% in variance. bycoh variants sit in the same band with a flat profile across quantities (the
per-cohort φ̂_g from ~6 base obs is leverage-biased too, and doesn't average away within cohorts);
d2_bycoh_cu's 0.68 "WRONG" is a band-edge case of this same mechanism, not a third channel.

### (d) d4/d5 (all-treated staggered) + cu/rb: ~0.86–1.11
Nothing was repaired — the composition changed. With every cohort treated, ALL non-base obs sit in
saturated cells, so the pooled φ̂ is identified essentially off the 180 base-period obs → V_base is
large and becomes the dominant share of the truth (verify from the ci rows: horizons 0.61–0.76 →
V_base ≈ 40–55% of horizon truth; main/pre ≈ 1.0 → ~100% of aggregate truth). The φ̂ component's
robust/CR variance is nearly unbiased; only the shrinking minority slope share carries the κ bias:
predicted horizon ratio ≈ sqrt(0.45 + 0.6·0.55) ≈ 0.88, observed 0.86–0.97. "All-treated repairs
the understatement" = the mis-estimated component became a minority of the estimand's variance.

### (e) d4/d5 + cluster(i): main/pre ~1.0, horizons ~0.6–0.76, partial blackouts
**Probe 2 (run), rep 71910003: all ten reported ame SEs = .2934876487 = SE_cl(pooled φ̂) from the
visible-ringer refit = .29348765, exact; V_es_ame eigenvalues {.689, ~1e-17} — the d1 collapse
verbatim.** The twist is pure composition: that single number ≈ the truth of main/pre ame (whose
slope noise averages over 120–180 cells and whose truth is V_base-dominated) while covering only
~60–75% of horizon truths. The most degenerate V in the grid produces the most accurate headline SE
in the table — by coincidence of aggregation, not by validity (cross-horizon contrasts still carry
exactly zero reported variance).
Blackouts: the CSVs show they are ALL-OR-NOTHING per rep (d4: 95/256 reps with all 10 SEs missing
and no e(V_es_ame); zero partial reps; d1c: 33/256; d1/d2/d5: 1–2/256). Probe 2's blackout rep
(71910001) gets the honest e(V)-preserve caution: "standard errors were not identified for 20 of 20
reported estimates, so no variance-covariance matrix e(V) was posted" — the whole table, att
included. Structurally, the all-treated staggered design is over-parameterized: at every calendar
period with no base-period obs the time FE is collinear with that period's cell dummies — at least
the 9 no-base-period constraints (11 drops observed; both probe reps show the same 11 cohort-30
treated-cell dummies dropped by reghdfe). The refusal screen then
fires stochastically across reps — an honest response by numerical roulette (37% of reps), while
the other 63% receive the silent collapsed V. Which numerical event flips a rep is a code-auditor
question; the theorist point is that the trigger is data-noise, not the design predicate, so the
same design gets two different handlings at random.

### (f) d1c: cluster(i) WRONG at 0.18–0.46, robust FLAG at 0.75–1.05
The cell-common shock is constant within every (g,t) cell, and the saturated cell intercepts θ_gh
absorb it COMPLETELY — it never reaches the AME's sampling error. Table proof, no probe needed: the
cross-rep truth SDs scale by the idiosyncratic σ: est-SD(d1c)/est-SD(d1) = 0.077/0.116–0.195/0.317
≈ 0.60–0.66 ≈ σ_idio = 0.6. So (i) robust is nearly right — its remaining horizon deficit
(0.75–0.89) is the same channel-B leverage bias, and main/pre hit 1.00–1.05; and (ii) cluster(i),
though it names the true dependence level, is destroyed by channel A (same 0.2-shaped profile as
d1: 0.46/0.39 main/pre, ~0.2 horizons) and buys nothing, because the dependence it guards against
has been moved from the errors into estimated fixed effects. The textbook "cluster at the level of
the shock" intuition fails under Mundlak saturation: the design conditions the shock out, and
clustering at that level then saturates the clusters and annihilates the CRVE. The correct
VCE advice for d1c is robust (or unit clustering) — or a design-based cross-cohort estimator.

## 2. Bucket classification (all 26 MC cases; buckets verbatim from the prereg)

Bucket (iii) — a correct SE is possible but the code posts something incorrect (20):
- (iii)-A, silent CRVE annihilation: d1_basic_ci, d2_basic_ci, d5_within_ci, d4_within_ci
  (posting reps; its blackout reps are the honest handling), d1c_basic_ci, d1_bytime_ci,
  d2_bytime_ci. Posted SEs are the SE of the absorbed baseline block only (probe-verified);
  understatement 1.4–5×; contrasts get ~zero variance; byTime posts a full-rank V that defeats

- (iii)-B, leverage-biased plug-in: d1_basic_cu, d1_basic_rb, d1_bycoh_cu, d1_bycoh_rb,
  d1_bytime_cu, d1_bytime_rb, d2_basic_cu, d2_basic_rb, d2_bycoh_cu (band-edge WRONG, same
  mechanism), d2_bycoh_rb, d2_bytime_cu, d2_bytime_rb, d1c_basic_rb. Systematic 10–30% SE
  understatement concentrated where within-cell slope noise dominates; correct alternatives exist
  (CR2/CR3/HC3, jackknife, bootstrap).

Bucket (iv) — no correct SE estimable in principle, yet something posted: EMPTY in this table.
Every case admits a correct SE under its own sampling model: with 15–30 cohorts, the per-cohort AME
components γ̂_gh are independent across cohorts, so the cross-cohort dispersion of per-cohort AMEs
(equivalently a pairs cluster bootstrap of the margins functional over cohorts) estimates every
Var(b_h) and every cross-horizon covariance — including for byCohort + cluster(i). The genuinely
in-principle-hopeless design (d3, dose constant within cohort: the estimand's identifying variation
is zero) was demoted to structure-only, and wooldid already refuses it with identification cautions
— nothing incorrect is posted there either.

NO-SE cases — d1_bycoh_ci, d2_bycoh_ci (2): classified (iii) by ceiling (a correct estimator
exists), but the OBSERVED handling — refuse all SEs, keep b, print an explicit caution — is the
RIGHT response for a package that only offers plug-in CRVE. It is strictly the best behavior in the
cluster(i) column: byCohort+cluster(i) saturates each cluster completely (φ̂_g is within-cluster),
the CRVE variance is exactly zero, the screen catches it deterministically, and the user is told.
The complaint is only that the same screen misses the basic/byTime collapse (nonzero but bogus V)
and fires only stochastically on d4 — the screen tests numerical missing-ness, not the design.

## 3. Prescriptions per non-(i) group, in priority order (user-harm)

**P1. (iii)-A — kill the silent collapse (highest harm: silent, 1.4–5× wrong, exported).**
Detection predicate (cheap, deterministic): contreat() + every treated (cohort × relative-period)
cell inside a single cluster of cluster(). On fire: (1) do not display plug-in CRVE ame SEs — post
b + missing SEs + a named caution ("cluster(X) clusters at/above the treatment-cell level: the
cluster-robust covariance of continuous-treatment effects is degenerate; every ame SE reduces to
the SE of the absorbed baseline dose slope, and cross-horizon contrasts carry zero estimated
variance; use robust, a cluster variable finer than cohort×period cells, or the bootstrap option");
(2) suppress e(V_es_ame)/e(b_es_ame)'s V half (or post with an explicit degenerate flag) so
honestdid/lincom/test cannot silently ingest it; (3) apply the predicate REGARDLESS of
contreatcontrols type — byTime's full-rank V must not exempt it (probe 1), i.e. never use a rank
test as the guard. Constructive replacement (named): **cluster bootstrap of the margins functional
over cohorts, or its analytic twin, the cross-cohort dispersion estimator** — V̂(b_h, b_h') =
Σ_g w_gh w_g'h' widehat-Cov of the per-cohort AMEs γ̂_gh around their weighted means, dof G−1. It is
consistent under exactly the cohort-sampling model that motivates cluster(i), sees both the
within-cell noise and any genuine cross-cohort dependence, and is PSD by construction.

**P2. (iii)-B joint tests — code change.** The 6–8-dimensional whitened statistics (adversarial,
jointtests, roth Wald) amplify the ~40% variance understatement into 20–35% rejection at nominal 5%
(§5.2/5.4). Fix at the V, not the reference: CR3/jackknife or a cluster/wild bootstrap of the ES
vector for the joint tests, or refuse the joint p with a named message when min treated-cell size
is small (p-per-cell/n-per-cell diagnostic). A footnote cannot carry a 4–7× type-I inflation.

**P3. d4-class stochastic blackout → make it deterministic.** The honest 20-of-20 refusal should
fire on the design predicate (P1) — the same all-treated staggered design should not get an honest
refusal in 37% of samples and a silently collapsed V in the rest. Also print the structural
over-parameterization note (time FEs collinear with cell dummies at calendar periods lacking
base-period obs) rather than leaving reghdfe's arbitrary drops silent under safety(off).

**P4. (iii)-B pointwise SEs — option + caveat.** Offer CR2/HC2 (unit-level CR2; HC3 as the
conservative choice) for contreat designs, or document: "with few obs per treated cell, robust/
cluster-robust ame SEs are biased down ~15–25%; cells of ≥ ~20 obs or CR2 mitigate". Do not change
the default silently (re-bless blast radius); do print a small-cell warning with the cell-size
diagnostic.

**P5. (iii)-C upgrade path.** Keep the byCohort+cluster(i) refusal; wire it to the P1 dispersion/
bootstrap estimator so the refusal becomes an answer. Steer text for d1c-type users: cell-common
shocks are absorbed by the saturated cell dummies — clustering at the shock level is unnecessary
for the ame quantities and mechanically fatal to plug-in CRVE; use robust/unit clustering or the
bootstrap.

## 4. Threshold ruling on the 0.75–0.85 FLAG band (robust/cluster(unit), d1/d2)

Split ruling. For DISPLAYED pointwise SEs: documentation caveat + runtime small-cell warning + an
opt-in CR2/HC3 switch — not a mandatory default change. The bias is real (~8–12 points of CI
under-coverage: nominal 95% → ~87–91%), design-guaranteed rather than incidental, but it is the
known behavior of the conventional estimator the option names, and silently changing defaults has a
larger blast radius than the harm. For the JOINT tests built on those V's: the answer CHANGES —
20–35% rejection at nominal 5% in the feature's advertised use case is a defect, not a caveat;
remedy per P2 (corrected/bootstrapped V or refusal). The asymmetry is principled: a t-interval
degrades gracefully with a mildly-short SE; an 8-dimensional whitened quadratic form does not.

## 5. Three most surprising results + the single probe that nails each

**S1. byTime + cluster(i): a full-rank, horizon-varying, plausible-looking V that contains zero
within-cell information.** Textbook intuition (and the package's own screens) treat rank/degeneracy
as the tell for a broken CRVE; here rank is fully restored while the annihilation is untouched.
Probe (RUN, probe_bytime): visible `ibn.t#c.ringer` refit — every reported horizon SE equals
SE_cl(φ̂_t) to all displayed digits. Nailed.

**S2. All-treated staggered + cluster(i): the grid's most degenerate V yields the grid's most
accurate headline SEs (main/pre ≈ 1.0), with a 37% stochastic honest blackout beside it.** Probe
(RUN, probe_d4): posting rep — all ten SEs = SE_cl(pooled φ̂) = .29348765 exactly, eigenvalues
{.689, ~1e-17}; blackout rep — "not identified for 20 of 20" caution, no e(V); both reps share the
same 11 structural collinearity drops, so the blackout trigger is numerical, not design-level.
Nailed except the exact per-rep trigger (assigned: code auditor).

**S3. d1c: clustering at the conceptually correct level is the worst available choice, and robust —
"wrong" under cell-correlated shocks — is nearly right.** The saturated cell intercepts absorb the
cell-common shock, so the AME truth scales with σ_idio only. Nailed from the table without a probe:
est-SD(d1c)/est-SD(d1) ≈ 0.60–0.66 ≈ σ_idio = 0.6 across all 10 quantities. The confirming single
probe, if wanted: refit one d1c rep adding the realized shock as a regressor — the ame estimates
should be numerically unchanged (shock lies in the span of the cell dummies), while robust SEs move
only trivially.
