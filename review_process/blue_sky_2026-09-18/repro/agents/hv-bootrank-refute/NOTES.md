(r1b runs the post cases at the top level; r1c runs the repost cases inside an eclass

|----------------------------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------|

| missing value on the diagonal          | **rc 504**              | **rc 504**              |
| finite negative diagonal (-0.004)      | rc 0, lincom se = .     | (same family as above)  |
| bootstrap-shaped rank 2 of 5           | rc 0; `test c1..c5` -> df 2, "Constraint N dropped" |

=> On this Stata, post and repost are INDISTINGUISHABLE on VCE screening. The comment's
"`ereturn repost' does NOT re-run `ereturn post''s positive-definiteness screen - MEASURED,
a 2x2 with V[1,1] = -1e100 reposts rc 0" is true of repost and equally true of post.
My r504 case is the refinement the prober did not have: the one screen post DOES apply
(missing values) repost applies too. The real reason repost is the right call is that
`ereturn post` wipes the rest of e() (and MOVES its source matrices - my r1b A left b and V
gone, which is why every case has to rebuild them).

hv-bootrank-01 sub-claims (post accepts rank-deficient PSD; `test` announces drops) and
hv-bootrank-02 (both halves) therefore reproduce independently.
